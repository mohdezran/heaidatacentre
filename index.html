Location advantage

Why Bentong, Pahang

An hour from Kuala Lumpur, yet a world apart: cooler foothill air, a hydro-rich grid, one of Malaysia's most water-secure basins — and room to scale for decades.

Strategic proximity

68 km from KL city centre via the Karak Expressway (E8), with the ECRL's Bentong station linking the campus into the East Coast Rail Link corridor.

Resilient power

Dedicated 275 kV intake from the TNB transmission grid, in a hydro-endowed state on the national transmission backbone — with headroom for gigawatt-class growth.

Water security

Sited in the Pahang river basin — the largest in Peninsular Malaysia — with over 2,000 mm of annual rainfall supporting closed-loop and harvested-water cooling.

Cooler foothill climate

The Titiwangsa foothills near Genting run measurably cooler than the Klang Valley floor — lowering cooling energy and lifting free-cooling hours year-round.

Land to scale

Contiguous acreage above the flood plain, in one of the most seismically stable regions on Earth — master-planned for phased growth beyond 100 MW.

Talent & incentives

Within reach of the Klang Valley's engineering workforce, supported by MIDA digital-investment incentives and Pahang's pro-investment state agencies.

Sustainability

Prosperity that doesn't cost the forest.

Pahang holds the world's oldest rainforest. Building here is a responsibility before it is an advantage — so the campus is engineered to draw lightly on the land that hosts it.

100%

Renewable-matched energy targeted via hydro and solar PPAs under Malaysia's Corporate Green Power and CRESS programmes.

≤ 1.30

Design annualised PUE — liquid cooling and cooler foothill ambient air cut mechanical overhead well below regional norms.

< 0.4 L/kWh

Design WUE, with rainwater harvesting and closed-loop systems that give back zero-discharge days to the Bentong river.

10 ha

Committed on-site native reforestation and riparian buffer — planted with species from Pahang's own forest reserves.
